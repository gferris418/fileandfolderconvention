# 05 — Project Structure

## Normative project template

```text
[PROJECT_CODE]_[PROJECT_NAME]/
├── 01_PROJECT_CONTROL/
├── 02_CONTRACT_COMMERCIAL/
├── 03_ENGINEERING/
├── 04_PROCUREMENT_LOGISTICS/
├── 05_SITE_EXECUTION/
├── 06_QA_QC_HSE/
├── 07_FINANCE/
├── 08_CORRESPONDENCE/
├── 09_TRAINING/
├── 10_HANDOVER_CLOSEOUT/
└── 99_ARCHIVE/
```

## Guidance by space

### 01_PROJECT_CONTROL
Project charter, plans, schedules, registers, meeting records, action tracking, reporting, and controlled project-management artifacts.

### 02_CONTRACT_COMMERCIAL
Contracts, amendments, commercial correspondence, change control, claims, payment milestones, and contractual deliverables.

### 03_ENGINEERING
Requirements, designs, drawings, calculations, specifications, technical submittals, reviews, and approvals.

### 04_PROCUREMENT_LOGISTICS
Purchase documentation, supplier coordination, packing/shipping information, logistics, customs, and delivery evidence.

### 05_SITE_EXECUTION
Site plans, work records, installation evidence, daily coordination, commissioning, and field documentation.

### 06_QA_QC_HSE
Inspection/testing, non-conformance, quality records, safety documentation, permits, and compliance evidence.

### 07_FINANCE
Project-specific cost, invoice, reconciliation, and payment-support material. Enterprise accounting truth remains in the relevant ERP/accounting system.

### 08_CORRESPONDENCE
Formal and material project correspondence not better classified elsewhere. Email systems remain native repositories where applicable; EIOS may project messages canonically.

### 09_TRAINING
Training plans, materials, attendance, certificates, feedback, and supporting records.

### 10_HANDOVER_CLOSEOUT
Acceptance, punch lists, as-built information, final deliverables, warranties, manuals, certificates, closeout registers, and lessons learned.

### 99_ARCHIVE
Closed/historical projection only. Archive placement does not itself establish records or retention status.
